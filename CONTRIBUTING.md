# Contributing

Contributions may add a resource, correct a claim, improve the application, or
help review a language profile. Evidence and community knowledge are both
valuable, but they must be represented transparently.

For data contributions, include:

- the language or variety and any known identifiers;
- the exact field or resource being added or corrected;
- a primary source URL, citation, page/table locator, or documented community
  review;
- geographic scope and precision;
- publication/version and retrieval dates;
- licence, access requirements, and redistribution status;
- uncertainty, cross-border provenance, and naming notes;
- how you would like to be credited, or that you prefer no public name.

Do not submit copyrighted corpus text, restricted audio, credentials, private
correspondence, personal data, or sensitive locations. A maintainer will apply
the verification workflow before a contribution enters a public release.

Technical contributions should keep generated files out of source control,
add validation or tests for changed behavior, and preserve accessibility and
low-bandwidth support.
